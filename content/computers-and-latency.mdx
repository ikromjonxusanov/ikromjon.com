summary: "Understanding latency in computing."
---

- **1 ns** = 1 / 1,000,000,000 s  
  - Intel i7: ~100 instructions per ns

- **1 μs (microsecond)** = 1000 ns  
  - Read 500 KB from memory

- **1 ms (millisecond)** = 1000 μs  
  - ~2 ms: Disk seek time  
  - ~150 ms: Ping time (USA to Europe)
